# KORA

## Vision

KORA is a platform that helps users discover emerging local artists through 30-second audio capsules enriched with cultural context.

## Problem

Music streaming platforms provide access to millions of songs, but discovering relevant local artists is difficult due to information overload and generic recommendation algorithms.

## Solution

KORA enables users to quickly discover local talent by combining short audio previews, cultural information, and community-driven discovery.

## Target Users

- Music explorers
- Local artists
- Cultural ambassadors (future)
- Music scouts (future)

## Value Proposition

Discover local sound in less than 45 seconds.
